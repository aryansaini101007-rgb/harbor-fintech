## About

Harbor Fintech is a registered financial technology company dedicated to simplifying the international education journey through secure, technology-driven financial solutions.

Our platform brings together education financing and foreign exchange services into a unified ecosystem that enables students to pursue global education with greater confidence, transparency, and efficiency.
